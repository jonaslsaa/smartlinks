Acceptable Use

Smartlinks is a shared preview service for running small programs from URLs. Use it for legitimate automations without harming people, systems, or the service.

You may not use Smartlinks to

  • Phish, impersonate, deceive, defraud, harvest credentials, or collect data without authorization.
  • Create, deliver, or operate malware, exploits, or attempts to gain unauthorized access to devices, accounts, or systems.
  • Send spam; scan or attack third-party systems; act as an abusive relay or proxy; or evade rate limits, access controls, or other safeguards.
  • Serve or distribute unlawful material, or material that infringes another person’s privacy, intellectual-property, or other legal rights.
  • Escape the sandbox, exhaust shared resources, disrupt Smartlinks, or interfere with other users.

Enforcement

Traffic may be rate-limited, rejected, or blocked when we reasonably believe it violates this policy or presents a security, legal, operational, or abuse risk. The hosted runtime and this policy may change while Smartlinks is in preview.

Reporting abuse

To report suspected abuse, start a request through the Smartlinks repository. Do not include a live Smartlink, credentials, or other sensitive details in a public issue; state that you need to share an abuse report privately.
